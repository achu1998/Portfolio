🏠 LiveWell – Complete Elderly Care Companion

  • Core Tech: Streamlit, Python, Llama LLM (Ollama), Geofencing, Accelerometer, Maps API, YouTube, Music Player
  • Target Users: Older adults + Family caregivers for aging-in-place safety and wellbeing

LiveWell is a tablet-based smart companion that combines intuitive UI, location-based safety monitoring, automatic fall detection, and a contextual AI chatbot to help older adults live independently while keeping families informed and connected.

The app features four core pillars: accessible UI for daily routines, geofencing safety alerts, accelerometer-based fall detection, and a Llama-powered chatbot with real-world context awareness (weather, maps, YouTube, music).


Core Features Overview

📍 Geofencing Safety

Monitors movement within safe zones and alerts family if resident leaves home or enters unsafe areas. Perfect for wandering prevention.

📱 Fall Detection

Uses tablet accelerometer to detect sudden falls and trigger immediate emergency notifications to caregivers.

🤖 Contextual AI Chatbot

Llama-powered assistant understands weather, location, time-of-day, and personal context. Can play music, show YouTube videos, provide navigation, and offer weather-based advice.

🎨 Senior-Friendly UI

Large buttons, high-contrast colors, simple flows designed for limited dexterity and vision changes.
